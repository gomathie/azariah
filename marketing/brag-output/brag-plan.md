# Azaria brag plan

Made with the brag-slim workflow on 2026-11-23. Two cuts: English (19.0 s) and Twi (18.5 s), vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.

## Angle

A shop owner cannot say what the shop made today. Azaria answers in one look. One claim per scene, each one shown on the real app.

- **What it is:** a bookkeeping app for small shops, on the phone.
- **Who it is for:** shop owners and traders in Ghana who keep records in a notebook or not at all.
- **Hook:** "How much did your shop make today?" over a till figure that will not settle.
- **Highlights:** all the money in one place; a sale recorded in seconds; who owes you; reports as PDF or Excel; works without internet.
- **Close:** "Record today. Grow tomorrow." and "Start with Azaria today".
- **Tone:** `app-store`, with a punchy pace. Voice-over kept, at the owner's request, and faster than the earlier ads.
- **Look:** the app's teal `#0f766e`, amber `#fbbf24` for emphasis, system font, real screenshots with sample data.

## Storyboard

Music is 120 beats a minute in C major; one beat is 15 frames and every cut is on a beat. A soft swish and a chime in key land on each cut.

| # | Scene | English frames | Twi frames | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 60 | 75 | The question, word by word; a till figure spinning beside a question mark |
| 2 | Brand | 45 | 45 | The mark pops with a ring; "Meet Azaria." |
| 3 | Money | 60 | 60 | Home screen; the "Money available" card ringed in amber |
| 4 | Record | 60 | 60 | Add sale screen, scrolling slightly |
| 5 | Debts | 60 | 60 | Customers screen; the customer who owes ringed in amber |
| 6 | Reports | 75 | 75 | Reports screen, scrolling slightly |
| 7 | Trust | 60 | 60 | No-internet icon; "Works without internet."; "Your records stay private" |
| 8 | Close | 150 | 120 | Mark, name, tagline, call to action pulsing on the beat |

Frame 0 is the closing scene, settled, so it is the thumbnail everywhere.

## Notes

- The names and phone numbers on the Customers screen are sample records from the local demo shop, not real customers.
- The English voice is Kokoro at speed 1.3. The Twi voice is Meta's MMS Akan model sped up 1.25 times with the pitch kept. Nobody who speaks Twi has checked the Twi wording or the voice.
- Nobody has listened to these files; they were made by a tool that cannot hear.
