# About Azaria

Azaria is a simple daily record book for small businesses. It helps you write down what you sell and what you spend, see the money you have, and know who owes you.

## The story behind Azaria

Azaria began with a question that many hard-working traders cannot answer at the end of the day: **how much did my business really make today?**

Across markets, kiosks, chop bars and workshops, people work from early morning until night. They sell, they buy stock, they give goods on credit to customers they trust, and they move money between cash, Mobile Money and the bank. Most of this lives in a notebook, on scraps of paper, or only in memory.

Then the notebook gets wet or lost. A customer's debt is forgotten. Business money and house money mix. A good month and a bad month feel the same, because nothing shows the difference. The business owner is working hard but cannot see clearly, and it is hard to grow what you cannot see.

Tools for keeping accounts already exist, but most were made for accountants and offices. They ask for a chart of accounts before you can record one sale. They need a steady internet connection. They use words that have to be learned first.

Azaria was built the other way round. It starts from the trader and the phone already in their hand, and from five questions a business owner asks every day.

## Why the name Azaria

The name comes from **Azariah**, one of the three young men in the book of Daniel. Azariah, Hananiah and Mishael, also known as Abednego, Shadrach and Meshach, were thrown into a blazing furnace because they would not give up what they believed. They walked in the fire and came out unharmed. Not a hair was singed, and their clothes did not even smell of smoke.

The name Azariah means "the Lord has helped".

That story is in our logo: the letter **A** with a flame standing inside it. The flame is there, and the A still stands.

Running a small business can feel like that furnace. Prices rise, customers pay late, and some months are hard. Azaria is named for coming through the fire with what you built still standing. Clear records are part of how a business does that: they show you where you are, so pressure does not turn into loss. And the Lord will help everyone to acheive success.

Azaria is for every entrepreneur, whatever their faith or background.

## Five questions, every day

1. How much did I sell?
2. How much money did I actually receive and spend?
3. How much cash or mobile money do I have now?
4. Who owes my business money, and how much?
5. Is my business improving, and what is driving the change?

Everything in Azaria exists to answer one of these.

## What we believe

- **Simple before sophisticated.** You should be able to record your first sale within minutes, with no training in accounting.
- **Correct before attractive.** Your figures must be right. Azaria never shows a "profit" number it cannot stand behind.
- **Made for the phone you have.** It works on affordable phones, on small screens and on a weak connection.
- **Works without internet, and says so honestly.** You can keep recording when the network is down, and Azaria tells you what is saved only on your phone and what is safely stored.
- **Your records are yours.** Only people you add can see your shop. Nobody else sees your business, and the support team can look only if you allow it.
- **For everyone.** Azaria welcomes every entrepreneur. Any learning content is optional.

## Record today, grow tomorrow

A record kept every day becomes something you can use. It shows which goods move, which costs are rising, and which customers pay on time. It helps you speak with confidence to a supplier, a lender or a mentor. A small habit today is what makes room for growth tomorrow.

## Who Azaria is for

- Market traders and shop owners who sell many small items.
- Food sellers and caterers.
- Artisans and service providers who are paid per job.
- Owners with staff, who want others to record sales while they keep control.
- People learning to run a business, who want a clear picture of their numbers.

## What Azaria is not

Azaria is a daybook. It is not a bank, it does not hold your money, and it does not file taxes for you. Its reports help you understand your business; they are not audited accounts.
