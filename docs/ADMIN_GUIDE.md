# Admin guide

For the shop owner and for anyone the owner has made an admin. It covers what staff cannot do. Read the user guide first for everyday work.

## Who can do what

| Task | Owner | Admin | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record sales, expenses, payments, transfers | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add and edit customers | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See reports and download them | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Undo an entry | Yes | Yes | No |
| Record owner money | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add people to the shop | Yes | Yes | No |
| Download all of the shop's data | Yes | No | No |
| See when support opened the shop | Yes | Yes | No |

A viewer can only read.

## Set up the shop

- When you create the shop you choose its name, type, country, currency and time zone. Azaria creates a Cash account and a Mobile Money account and a starter list of categories.
- **Business settings** in Settings shows the name, country, currency and time zone. They cannot be changed in the app yet; ask support if one is wrong.
- The time zone decides which day an entry belongs to. A sale just before midnight counts for that day in your time zone.
- The currency is fixed when the shop is created.

## People and phones

Open Settings, then **Staff and access**.

- **Add a person**: enter their name and the phone they use, and choose **Staff** or **Admin**.
- **Staff** record sales and expenses. **Admin** can also correct mistakes and add people.
- The last owner of a shop cannot be removed or demoted, so a shop is never left without an owner.

## Fix a mistake

Entries are never deleted. To correct one:

1. Open the daybook and tap the entry.
2. Say why you are reversing it, then tap **Reverse this entry**.

The original stays in the daybook, crossed out and marked Reversed, and a reversal entry puts the money back. A credit sale that already has a payment against it cannot be reversed until that payment is reversed first.

## Owner money

When you put your own money into the business, or take money out for yourself, tap **Add**, then **Owner money**. It changes the account balance. It is not a sale and not an expense, so it does not distort either figure.

## Money accounts

Settings, **Money accounts** lists each account and its balance. Tap one for its statement. **Move money** and **Owner money** are also there.

## Reports for decisions

- Compare **Sales summary** with **Money received** to see how much you sell on credit.
- **Customer debts** shows who to follow up.
- **Operating expenses** by category shows where the money goes.
- **Cash movement** explains why the money available changed.
- Download a report as PDF to share it, or as Excel to work on the numbers.

## Keep your data safe

- **Download all my data** in Settings gives the owner a full copy of the shop's records as a file. Do it regularly and keep it somewhere safe.
- Ask everyone to tap **Sync** before the end of the day so nothing stays only on a phone.
- Each person signs in with their own email. Do not share one password.

## Privacy and support access

- Only people you add, and the Azaria support team, can see your shop. Other shops cannot see yours.
- The support team can open your shop to help you. Some team members can only look. Some can also add or correct entries.
- An entry made by support is saved under that support person's name, not yours.
- Settings, **Privacy and data**, **What happened** lists each time support opened your shop.

## For the Azaria support team

This part is for platform support staff.

- Open the round button at the top right and choose **Admin console**.
- **Overview** shows the counts: shops, active shops, accounts, people who signed up without making a shop, and whether payments are on.
- **Shops**: search by shop name or owner email. You see the shop's status, people and last entry date.
- **Open to look** shows the shop as the owner sees it, with a banner. You cannot record or change anything.
- **Open to make changes** lets you do what the owner can do. What you add or change is saved under your own name. You see this button only if you have the right.
- A shop stays open for two hours, or until you tap **Leave** or reload the page.
- **Suspend shop** closes a shop to its members. **Reactivate shop** opens it again. Both need a reason.
- **Payments for this shop** is an on/off switch. On means this shop must pay; off means it is free. A shop you have not touched follows the general switch and says "Same as everyone". One tap sets the shop apart; **Go back to the same as everyone** removes that.
- **Accounts** lists everyone who signed up, newest first, with their shops. Search by email.
- **Team** is for the superadmin. Add a person by the email of their Azaria account and tick what they may do: open shops and look, add or change anything in shops, suspend shops, or manage discount codes, payments and prices. A change works at once. A superadmin can do everything and cannot change their own entry.
- Every search, opening and change is recorded, and the owner can see when their shop was opened.
- Choose **Discount codes** in the Admin console to give someone money off Azaria Plus. Type a code of 6 to 24 letters or numbers, the percent off, and how long it works: 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or for ever. You can also limit how many payments may use it.
- Choose **Payments** in the Admin console to turn payments on or off for every shop at once. Payments start off: nobody is asked to pay and every shop uses everything for free. When you turn them on, every shop is asked to pay from that moment, and you are asked to confirm first. On the same screen you can change the price of each plan or stop offering one.
- A 100% code turns Plus on with nothing to pay. Give such a code only to the person it is meant for, and limit it to one payment.
- **Switch off** stops a code at once. Time a shop already got with the code stays.
- The shop list shows until when each shop has Plus. You cannot turn Plus on for a shop yourself; give the owner a code.
