# User guide

Everything you can do in Azaria day to day. Read it in the app under Profile, Guides.

## Getting started

- **Create an account** with your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
- **Sign in** on any phone or computer with the same email and password.
- **Forgot your password?** Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. A link is sent to your email.
- **Confirm your email.** After you sign in, Azaria asks you to confirm your email. Tap "Send me the code", open the email, and type the code. You can tap "Later" and keep working; Azaria asks again after three days.
- **Change language** in Profile. Azaria is in English and French.
- **Put Azaria on your home screen**: open the round button at the top right, then tap "Add to home screen". It then opens like an app.

## Home

Home answers the questions you ask every day.

- **Money available** is the money in your accounts right now: Cash, Mobile Money, and Bank if you use it. Tap an account to see what went in and out.
- **Quick actions** take you straight to a sale, an expense, a customer payment, or moving money.
- **Today, This week, This month** change the period for the six figures below them.
- **Sales** is the value of what you sold. **Money received** is what actually came in. They differ when a customer buys on credit.
- **Operating expenses paid** and **Stock purchases paid** are kept apart, because goods you bought to sell are not the same as running costs.
- **Net cash movement** is money in minus money out for the period.
- **Customer debts** is what customers owe you now. Tap it to see who.
- **Recent entries** are your last five entries.

Sales minus spending is not your profit. Stock you still hold, credit sales and your own money change the picture.

## Record a sale

1. Tap **Add**, then **Add sale**.
2. Enter the sale amount.
3. Choose **Paid in full**, **Partly paid**, or **Not paid yet**.
4. If money came in, choose where: Cash, Mobile Money or Bank.
5. If the customer still owes you, choose their name, or add them on the spot.
6. Say what you sold if you want to remember it. Tap **Save**.

The part not paid is added to what that customer owes.

## Record an expense

1. Tap **Add**, then **Add expense**.
2. Choose **Expense** for running costs such as rent, transport and airtime, or **Bought stock** for goods you bought to sell.
3. Enter the amount, the account you paid from, and a category.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Customers and what they owe

- **Add a customer**: open Customers and tap **Add customer**. A phone number is optional.
- **Edit a customer**: tap **Edit** next to their name.
- **See what a customer owes**: tap their name. You see each credit sale, each payment, and what they still owe.
- **Record a payment**: on the customer's page tap **Record payment**, or tap **Add**, then **Record payment** and choose the customer. Enter the amount and where the money went.
- A payment lowers what the customer owes. It is not a new sale, so your sales do not go up twice.
- You cannot record more than the customer owes.
- **Send a reminder**: if the customer has a phone number, "Send reminder" opens WhatsApp with a polite message. You decide whether to send it.

## Move money between accounts

When you pay cash into Mobile Money or the bank, tap **Add**, then **Move money**. Choose the two accounts and the amount. Your sales and expenses do not change; only the two balances do.

## The daybook

The daybook lists everything, newest first, grouped by day.

- A **plus** and green mean money came in. A **minus** and red mean money went out.
- Each entry shows the customer and the account. A credit sale shows what is not yet paid.
- Tap an entry to see its details.
- **Sync** at the top sends anything saved on this phone.

## Invoices and receipts

- **Invoice**: tap **Add**, then **Invoice**. It records the sale and makes a bill with the customer's name that you can download as a PDF. It is not a second sale.
- **Receipt**: tap **Add**, then **Receipt**. Use it for a sale paid now, or pick a sale or payment already in the daybook. Download it as a PDF.

## Reports

Open Reports and choose one:

- **Sales summary**: sales for each day, with a total.
- **Money received**: by account.
- **Operating expenses** and **Stock purchases paid**: by category.
- **Account statement**: one account, with opening balance, money in, money out and closing balance.
- **Cash movement**: every movement, with transfers shown and not counted.
- **Customer debts**: who owes you and how much.

Choose the dates, then tap **Download PDF** or **Download Excel** to keep or share the report.

## Working without internet

- You can save a **sale** or an **expense** with no connection. Azaria says "Saved on this device".
- When you are back online, open the daybook and tap **Sync**, or open Sync status from Settings.
- A payment, a transfer, an invoice PDF and reports need a connection.
- An entry saved only on the phone can be lost if you clear the browser's data or lose the phone. Sync as soon as you can.

## Your profile and menu

The round button at the top right opens your menu: your shop, another shop if you belong to more than one, Profile, Settings, Money accounts, Help and Sign out.

## What you cannot do as staff

Staff record sales, expenses, payments and transfers. Staff cannot undo an entry, record owner money, add people, or download the whole business. Ask the owner or an admin.
