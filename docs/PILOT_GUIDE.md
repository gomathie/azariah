# Pilot guide

This is the guide for a shop owner trying the local app. It is not a hosted pilot yet.

## Start

1. Open Azaria and create an account.
2. Name the business, and check the country, currency, and time zone.
3. Record today's sales and the money you actually received.
4. If someone pays later, open Customers and record the payment. Do not add it as a new sale.
5. Use Move money when you move cash to Mobile Money.
6. If the phone is offline, save the sale or expense anyway, then tap Sync when you are back online.

## What to remember

- Sales minus spending is not your profit. Home says this next to the numbers.
- An entry saved only on the phone can disappear if the browser data is cleared.
- Only an owner can download all of the business data. Settings → Download all my data.

## Feedback to collect later
